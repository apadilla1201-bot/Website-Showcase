Questions

The things people ask before they book.

How fast do you actually reply?

Within fifteen minutes, day or night, by a person. Most quotes arrive sooner. Tell us if you need to fly today and we start with the aircraft closest to you.

Are there hidden fees?

No. The quote includes positioning, crew, overnight charges, airport fees and catering. The invoice matches the quote.

Can you fly today?

Usually, yes. With 2,400 aircraft on call, a departure within four hours is common on major routes.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel free up to 48 hours before departure on most aircraft. The terms are on the quote, in plain words, before you confirm.

Will the aircraft be swapped for an older one?

The aircraft on your quote is the aircraft you fly. If an operator needs to change it, we tell you first and you choose.
