How we work

The best work is the work nobody notices.

Patients tell us the same three things. They are scared of looking done. They have been upsold before. They felt rushed. So we built the clinic around the opposite.

  1. 01

    Start small.

    Wrinkle relaxers and fillers are dosed to the smallest amount that does the job. You come back at two weeks and we adjust, at no charge. More can always be added. Less cannot.

  2. 02

    Say the real price.

    Every number is on the table before anything begins, in writing. Follow-ups, touch-ups and aftercare are inside the quote, not added at the desk.

  3. 03

    Take the time.

    Consultations run 45 minutes with the doctor who treats you. Not a coordinator. Not a sales team. Nobody calls you afterwards to chase a decision.

The consultation

Forty-five minutes, no pressure.

Hold to take a minute.

Consultations are $150, credited to any treatment you choose.

  1. Minute 0

    You talk. We listen. What bothers you, what you have tried, what you are afraid of.

  2. Minute 10

    The doctor examines and explains, with a mirror and a pencil, what would help and what would not.

  3. Minute 30

    A written plan with every price on it. Nothing is missing from it later.

  4. Minute 45

    You go home and think about it. No deposit today. No follow-up calls.
